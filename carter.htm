                     Carter Family Cemetery

Located Geneva, Geneva Co., AL, from Jct. 52 & 27 go 1 mile north on
highway 27, right on highway 85, for 1.05 mile, cemetery to the right,
within a chain link fence.  Copied by Mona C. Spears and Annie Ruth Carter
on 15 August 1977.
