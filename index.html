Local Sources

  • Probate Judge Office of Geneva Co.
    P.O. Box 430
    Geneva, AL 36340

    The courthouse is located at 200 N. Commerce street and has records from 1898 forward. Some records (1898–1929) have been damaged by floods.

  • Emma Knox Kenan Library
    312 S. Commerce St.
    Geneva, AL 36340

    One of the best resources for the county are the cemetery records compiled by Mr. and Mrs. J.R. Phipps, 1962–1972. A copy of the two volume set is available here and at the Alabama Department of Archives and History.

    The library also has the Reaper newspaper, original and on microfilm from about 1900 forward.
